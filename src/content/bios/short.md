Daren Tan is the Founder and CEO of ALPHV Group of Companies, which builds AI, data and cloud solutions for enterprises and government across Southeast Asia. A technical founder with 10 years in the industry, he was previously at BASF and MaGIC and CTO of startups in Singapore, China and Malaysia. He founded Developer Kaki, Malaysia’s largest developer community, with 82,000+ members.
