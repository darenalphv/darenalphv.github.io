Daren Tan is the Founder and CEO of ALPHV Group of Companies, which builds AI, data and cloud solutions for enterprises and government across Southeast Asia, and trains and recruits the tech talent to run them. A technical founder with 10 years in the industry, he started as a developer and serial hackathon competitor, became CTO of startups in Singapore, China and Malaysia, and built AI solutions at BASF and MaGIC. As Director of Technology at the accelerator Growth Charger, he designed technical due-diligence frameworks and advised founders on architecture and AI. He founded Developer Kaki, Malaysia’s largest developer community with 82,000+ members, and co-founded Hackathon Kaki. Under his leadership, ALPHV became the first Malaysian Global Strategic Partner of Alibaba Cloud. He holds a master’s in data science, has judged 20+ hackathons and gave the technical AI keynote at the ASEAN AI Summit 2025.
