## Direction contract

THESIS: Daren's decade reported the way a Bursa-listed group reports itself: a cover, key figures with notes, a group structure, a profile of the founder, recognition and media. Proof arrives in the register boardrooms already trust. It refuses the speaker-site default (portrait hero, services cards, testimonial carousel, "book me" form).

OWN-WORLD: White report stock (#F8F8F8 ground, #0D0D0F ink) and one committed ultramarine (#1B2BD9 family, sampled #0921CC). Ultramarine owns the full-bleed section plates and the LinkedIn action, never decoration. Type is one grotesque family, Archivo variable: Black/heavy wide cuts for display and figures, regular widths for text, with tabular numerals throughout. Stage photography bleeds to its own black and is never boxed or rounded. Hairline 1px rules divide the figures. Outlined rectangles with solid or dashed connectors form the group structure. Every figure carries a superscript note number that resolves to a Notes list naming its source. There are no cards, shadows, gradients, glass or rounded corners.

STORY: The visitor sees the name, the title and the ALPHV group on an ultramarine cover beside Daren on stage, then reads four key figures (72,000+, 4, 10, 30+), each traceable to a note. Next they see how the group fits together, read the founder's profile and track record, see the recognition and media appearances, and leave through "Connect on LinkedIn". The case-study pages read as segment reviews.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin white bar holds the "Daren Tan" wordmark at left, five section links at right and a solid ultramarine "Connect on LinkedIn" button at the far right. The cover splits 60/40 below the bar. Left is the ultramarine plate: "Report 2016 to 2026" at top-left, "Daren Tan" in white Archivo Black at a 128.8px cap height (on the 2688 comp), the two-line title "Founder & Chief Executive Officer, ALPHV Group of Companies" below it, and the caption "Cover art: 72,000 points, one for every Developer Kaki member" at bottom-left. Under the cover sits a white key-figures strip with four tabular numerals and hairline rules, then the "Group Structure" heading and the top of the chart.

FORM: An annual report of a listed group, item 6 of my ordered grounded list. Seed key d0b89fc7. The signature interaction is the cover's 72,000-point field (OGL), whose points drift and gather on the pointer. Motion grammar: figures roll digit by digit in fixed tabular positions when they enter view; group-structure connectors draw in solid or dashed strokes; Lenis smooths scroll; everything is visible by default, with a full reduced-motion path.
