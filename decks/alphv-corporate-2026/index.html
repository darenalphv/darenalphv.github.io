Situation

Damage claims arrive as images, video and PDFs, at any hour. With Tasco Insurance in Vietnam, ALPHV built a proof of concept that assesses them around the clock, from the first photo to the claim report.

Approach

A multimodal case system that prepares the liability assessment and claim report.
