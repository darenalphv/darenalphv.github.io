WhatsApp AI agents cut manual data entry time by 80% for a Singapore hospitality group

Agentic automationSingapore2026 QwenWhatsApp
Situation

A leading hospitality group in Singapore talks to its customers on WhatsApp. Leads from those conversations were entered into the CRM by hand.

Approach

An AI CRM with WhatsApp sales agents.

  • Agents handle customer conversations 24/7, in natural language
  • Every lead syncs to the CRM, and hot leads reach sales in real time
  • Intelligent conversation flows with guardrails
  • A CRM dashboard by user access level, with one-click contracts, invoices and reports

How it runs. The agents run on Qwen inside WhatsApp, where the conversations already happen.
