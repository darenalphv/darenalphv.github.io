A proof of concept assesses insurance damage claims from images and video, 24/7

Agentic automationVietnamProof of concept, 2026 QwenMultimodal AI
Situation

Damage claims arrive as images, video and PDFs, at any hour. With Tasco Insurance in Vietnam, ALPHV built a proof of concept that assesses them around the clock, from the first photo to the claim report.

Approach

A multimodal case system that prepares the liability assessment and claim report.

  • Images, video and PDFs in one case file
  • Duplicate claims from the same person identified automatically
  • Liability assessment based on Vietnam’s Road Act and compendium
  • Third-party garage, workflow, email and WhatsApp automation

How it runs. Qwen models read images, video and documents together, and AI agents move each case to the claim report.
